Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Photos and local processing

The photo editor processes selected images in your browser using client-side JavaScript and the browser canvas API. This static website does not upload photos to a photo-processing server. Your browser temporarily holds image data while you use the editor.

Downloads

When you choose a download, your browser creates the output file locally. You control where that file is saved.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider (such as GitHub Pages or Vercel) may process standard technical request data under its own policies. If you add analytics, advertising, contact forms or third-party scripts later, update this policy to accurately describe those services and their data practices.

Children and sensitive documents

Do not upload or share sensitive documents unnecessarily. Use the site only where lawful and appropriate, and close the page when finished on a shared device.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website changes. This template is general information, not legal advice; adapt it to your actual site operations and applicable laws.
