ABOUT US

Simple photo preparation, made accessible

Document Photo Studio is a browser-based tool designed to help people prepare portrait photos for common document, ID, passport, visa and application formats.

The editor provides resizing, framing controls, solid-colour canvas backgrounds and downloadable print sheets. Image processing is performed in your browser; this static website does not send your selected photo to our server.

Important limitations

We are not a government agency, embassy, exam board or document-issuing authority. Presets are helpful starting points, not a guarantee of acceptance. Always check the latest official instructions for dimensions, face positioning, background, file type and file size.
