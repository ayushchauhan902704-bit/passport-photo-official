Frequently asked questions

Are all listed dimensions officially accepted?

No. Some are commonly used standard dimensions and some are generic. The receiving government, embassy, exam portal or institution has the final requirements. Check its current instructions before submitting.

Does changing the background remove the old background?

No. This version fills the canvas with a solid colour behind the full uploaded image, but it does not automatically separate a person from the original background. True background removal requires a segmentation engine, which is not included here.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Image editing and export happen locally in your browser. Browser memory is used while the page is open; close the page to clear the working session.

Can I meet a specific KB limit?

The optional target asks the JPG exporter to try different quality levels. Results vary by image and the requested target may not be achievable without reducing dimensions.

What is the recommended pixel resolution?

For print sizes, the tool calculates pixels from your selected DPI (300 DPI by default). Some online applications instead require fixed pixel dimensions and file-size limits.
